AnkiFlash (including the AnkiFlash Generator Chrome Extension and the AnkiFlash Add-on for Anki) is owned and operated by **Long Ly**, a developer from Vietnam. This policy outlines our commitment to your privacy through a "Privacy by Design" approach: **No Collection, No Accounts, No Tracking.**

### 1. User Accounts and Personal Information
You are **not required** to create a user account, provide an email address, or share any personal information to use AnkiFlash. We do not collect, store, or have access to any personally identifiable information (PII).

### 2. Data Processing and Dictionary Requests
AnkiFlash acts as an intermediary tool to help you study. 
* When you generate a flashcard, the extension sends direct requests to reputable online dictionaries (such as **Oxford, Cambridge, Collins, LacViet, Jisho, or Wiktionary**) to fetch definitions and audio. 
* These requests are handled directly between your device and the dictionary providers. We do not intercept or store the content of these queries on any external server.

### 3. Local Storage Only
All data related to your study sessions remains strictly on your local device. This includes:
* Input word lists.
* Successfully generated or failed cards.
* User configurations (selected dictionaries, translation options, and sound settings).

### 4. Log Files
AnkiFlash does **not** collect or transmit log files to us. Any operational logs are displayed exclusively within your browser's "Console" tab in Developer Tools for your own troubleshooting purposes.

### 5. Website Analytics Notice
The legal documentation for AnkiFlash is hosted on `longly.info`. While this website uses **Google Analytics (gtag.js)** to monitor general site traffic, the AnkiFlash extension itself does **not** contain any tracking code, cookies, or analytics scripts.

### 6. Consent
By using AnkiFlash, you hereby consent to this Privacy Policy.
